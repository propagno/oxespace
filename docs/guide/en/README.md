- [OXESpace your way](06-your-way.md): Every screen complete and fast, in your language, light or dark, by keyboard or screen reader.
- [All your agents in one place](07-agents.md): See and act on every agent: OxeCode panes, OxeChat conversations, delegations and the phone.
