# OXESpace 1.3.2

Fixes and small requests reported on 1.3.1. Installs of 1.3.x on Windows update automatically; on
Linux, install the new package (the AppImage updates itself).

## Conversations

- **A question's options have room to be read.** In the dock, the "Needs your input" card is
  compact: its title moves to the bar and the frame is tighter, so two whole options with their
  descriptions fit at 1280×800 while the conversation keeps at least 30% of the view. **Expand**,
  next to **Hide**, gives the request up to 72% of the view and is remembered.
- **Send a queued message now.** Each queued message has a **Send now** button. With no turn
  running, it is sent at once. While a turn runs, OXESpace asks before stopping it, then sends the
  message next. A stop you asked for this way does not pause the rest of the queue; any other
  interruption or failure still does. Before, a message queued during a long turn waited for the
  whole turn, and *Send next now* only appeared when the queue was paused.

## OxeQuery

- **Drag to resize** the query editor against its results, the MongoDB query fields against their
  results, the table or collection list (180–520 px) and the OxeQuery sidebar. Dividers also work
  with the arrow keys and Home/End, a double click restores the default size, and sizes are
  remembered. The results always keep at least 120 px. This replaces the editor's resize corner.

## Known limits

- The limits listed in [1.3.0](1.3.0.md) still apply.
