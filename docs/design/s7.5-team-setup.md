# S7.5 · Team setup that feels finished (design spec)

Status: draft for review · Owner: DESIGN/PRODUCT · Implements: S7.5 (FRONTEND screen, BACKEND contract gaps)
· Date: 2026-10-31 · Walked on origin/main 21fc9fab (`TeamDialog.tsx`, `TeamMessages.tsx`, `team.ipc.ts`,
`team-access.ts`, `team-tools.ts`), with #197 (translation), F35 (#319, entry points) and the coordinator
unification (#219, decision A) taken as landed.

## 1. Outcome

**The person builds a project team (members, coordinator, sessions, delivery) in a few minutes, without reading
a paragraph of rules. Afterwards they always know who is connected to what, whether messages are reaching them, and
who is the coordinator.** The user is about to do exactly this for the 1.4 team (6 members across OxeCode panes and
OxeChat conversations), so this is the first-time path.

## 2. The walk (first-time user, today)

| Step | What happens today | Problem | Checklist |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Open Team | Title-bar button (moves with F35 to Tools menu, palette, `Ctrl+Shift+T`) | fine after F35 | — |
| 2. Empty team | `<p>No members yet. Add a coordinator or another project role.</p>` under a paragraph of rules | no `EmptyState`; doesn't say what a team is *for*; rules come before the first action | 1, 2 |
| 3. Add a member | name field + 5 role radios + **Add member**; the new row appears | no feedback or focus move after adding; role descriptions missing ("Project manager" vs "Product manager"?) | 3, 5 |
| 4. Connect a session | **Connect current session** appears only when the dialog was opened *from* a pane or conversation, and connects **only that one** | to connect 6 members the person must focus each pane/conversation, reopen Team and click, 6 times; nothing says this is how it works | 3 |
| 5. See what's connected | row says "Session connected" / "Session disconnected · reconnect to authorize MCP" / "No session connected" | doesn't say **which** pane or conversation; "authorize MCP" is jargon; no way to open it | 2, 5, 6 |
| 6. Pick the coordinator | **Make coordinator** on every row, no confirmation; radio disabled with a note | with decision A it grants the project coordinator's powers (#219); it needs the same confirmation as #290 | 3, 6 |

## 3. Layout: list and detail

The `Dialog` (size `xl`) becomes two panes:

```
┌ Equipe do projeto · oxespace ──────────────────────────────────────── × ┐
│ ┌ Membros ───────────────┐ ┌ Frontend · Desenvolvedor ──────────────────┐│
│ │ ◉ Coordenadora  ★      │ │ Sessão   ● OxeCode · painel 2 · Claude  Abrir││
│ │ ● Frontend             │ │ Entrega  Pela caixa de entrada (OxeCode)    ││
│ │ ○ Backend   sem sessão │ │ Papel    Desenvolvedor ▾                    ││
│ │ ● Mobile               │ │ ───────────────────────────────────────────││
│ │ + Adicionar membro     │ │ Mensagens                                   ││
│ │ Arquivados (1) ▸       │ │ …                                           ││
│ └────────────────────────┘ └─────────────────────────────────────────────┘│
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Left, members** (`ListRow`, catalog audit #323): status dot (session), name, role, ★ for the coordinator,
  "sem sessão" / "no session" when unbound. Coordinator first, then the order the person added them. `↑↓` moves,
  `Enter` selects. Width 280 px; below 720 px of dialog width the panes stack (list, then detail with a back button).
- **Right, the selected member**: three facts with their one action each (Sessão, Entrega, Papel), then messages.
  Every per-member button moves here or into the row's ⋯ menu (Tornar coordenadora…, Arquivar), so the list is
  readable.
- The rules paragraph goes away; each rule appears where it applies (§5, §6).

## 4. Empty and first member

- **Empty team**: `EmptyState` (heading level 2):
  - title: "Monte a equipe deste projeto" / "Build this project's team"
  - body: "Uma equipe dá papéis aos seus agentes e um lugar para eles trocarem mensagens. Criar um membro não inicia
    nenhum agente." / "A team gives your agents roles and a place to message each other. Creating a member doesn't
    start an agent."
  - actions: **Adicionar membro** (primary) and **Usar uma equipe sugerida** (secondary): adds Coordenadora,
    Desenvolvedor, Revisor with editable names, in one step (IDEA, cost S; Linear and Notion offer starter templates).
- **Add member** opens an inline form at the end of the list: name (required, 100 chars) and role as a `Select`
  with one-line descriptions:
  - Coordenadora · "Conduz as outras conversas do projeto" (§6)
  - Gerente de projeto · "Acompanha prazos e dependências"
  - Gerente de produto · "Decide o que entra e por quê"
  - Desenvolvedor · "Implementa"
  - Revisor · "Revisa e aprova o trabalho"
  (en: Coordinator, Project manager, Product manager, Developer, Reviewer, with the same one-liners.)
  After adding: the new member is selected, focus goes to **Conectar uma sessão** (§5), and a polite live region says
  "Frontend adicionado" / "Frontend added".

## 5. Connecting a session (the biggest gap)

- The detail's **Sessão** row has **Conectar uma sessão…** / **Connect a session…**, which opens a picker listing
  **every live agent session of this project**: OxeCode panes with an agent and OxeChat conversations, each with
  provider icon, title, mode and state (the S7 vocabulary, `agents.state.*`). Sessions already bound to another member
  show that member's name and are disabled with "Já conectada a Backend". The current pane or conversation (when the
  dialog was opened from one) is first and marked "Esta sessão".
- No contract change: `team.connect(scope, revision, memberId, owner)` already takes any owner, and main
  (`team-access.ts` `connectFromUi`) checks that the session is live and belongs to the project. The picker reads
  candidates from the renderer stores; main stays the judge.
- From the other side: a pane's ⋯ menu and an OxeChat conversation's ⋯ menu get **Entrar na equipe como…** / **Join
  the team as…**, listing unbound members. This is how people coming from a session expect it (one click, no dialog).
- Connected: "● OxeCode · painel 2 · Claude" with **Abrir** (goes there) and ⋯ › **Desconectar**. Disconnected
  (session ended): "○ Sessão encerrada · conecte de novo para ela ler as mensagens" / "Session ended · connect again so
  it can read messages". Never "authorize MCP".
- An agent session that isn't running can't be picked; the picker's empty state says "Nenhuma sessão de agente
  aberta neste projeto. Inicie um agente no OxeCode ou uma conversa no OxeChat." with the two actions.

## 6. Coordinator (unified with B1.4, decision A)

- **Tornar coordenadora…** (row ⋯ and the role `Select`) opens the **same confirmation as #290**: Cancel focused,
  what the role sees and can do, "Ela nunca responde um pedido nem uma aprovação", and "Backend deixa de ser a
  coordenadora" when it replaces someone. One component, two entry points.
- Bound to an OxeChat conversation: full powers (the project coordinator). Bound to an OxeCode pane: the detail says
  "Num painel do OxeCode, a coordenadora só troca mensagens; as ferramentas de coordenação ficam nas conversas do
  OxeChat." / "In an OxeCode pane the coordinator only exchanges messages; the coordination tools are in OxeChat
  conversations." (or the choice is refused, per BACKEND's #219 decision; the copy follows it).
- One word everywhere after the unification: badge **Coordenadora** / **Coordinator** in the Team, the OxeChat
  sidebar and the S7 central. The glossary merges its two rows when #219 lands.

## 7. Delivery, as a state

The detail's **Entrega** row shows one of these, with its action:

| State | pt-BR | en | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| OxeChat, on | Automática · chega quando a conversa fica livre | Automatic · arrives when the conversation is free | Pausar |
| OxeChat, off | Pausada · as mensagens esperam na caixa de entrada | Paused · messages wait in the inbox | Ligar entrega automática |
| OxeChat, paused by the app | Pausada depois de {{reason}} · nada é reenviado sozinho | Paused after {{reason}} · nothing is resent on its own | Retomar entrega |
| OxeCode pane | Pela caixa de entrada · o agente lê pelas ferramentas da equipe | Through the inbox · the agent reads it with the team tools | — |
| No session | Sem sessão · as mensagens ficam guardadas | No session · messages are kept | Conectar uma sessão… |

`{{reason}}` is a C3 reason (falha, interrupção, reinício do app). The paused-by-the-app state also adds a
`needs-you` entry to the activity center ("A entrega para Frontend foi pausada") so it isn't discovered by accident.
**Contract need:** today `deliveryEnabled` is a boolean and the pause reason isn't exposed; BACKEND adds
`delivery: {state: 'on'|'off'|'paused', reason?: MainMessage}`.

## 8. Messages

- Each message: sender → recipient, **kind as a `Badge`** (Pedido, Progresso, Pergunta, Resposta, Resultado,
  Decisão / Request, Progress, Question, Answer, Result, Decision), body, time, and its delivery state in the S7 style
  (dot + words: "Recebida pelo agente", "Entregue à sessão · sem confirmação", "Entrega incerta · confira a sessão antes
  de reenviar", "Enviando…", "Guardada · ainda não lida").
- Live updates come from a main event instead of 5 s polling when S7.1's push exists. Until then polling stays, but
  new messages keep the scroll position and get a "{{n}} novas" pill (same rule as the S7 list: nothing jumps under
  the pointer).
- Composer: **Enviar** / **Send** (the inbox is how it works, not the label); `Ctrl+Enter` sends.
- **Messages to the person (contract gap).** `oxespace_team_message` needs `recipientId` to be a member, so an agent
  can't ask *you* a question or report a decision to you. CCR for BACKEND: a recipient `person` (the project's
  human), whose messages appear in the member's thread, in the activity center as `needs-you` (questions and
  decisions) or `finished` (results), and on the S7 central row of the sender. Agents still never answer approvals.

## 9. Archive, errors, loading

- **Archive**: immediate, with toast "Frontend arquivado · Desfazer" (5 s); the list's **Arquivados (n)** disclosure
  lists them with **Restaurar**. Archiving a connected member disconnects it first, and the toast says so.
- **Errors**: main's `Error` strings become C3 codes (BACKEND): `team.changed` ("A equipe mudou em outra janela;
  atualizamos a lista." — re-read automatically, no retry button), `team.sessionNotRunning`,
  `team.sessionOutsideProject`, `team.sessionTaken`, `team.deliveryNeedsConversation`,
  `team.conversationGone`. The renderer shows them with `formatMainMessage`, never `String(e)`.
- **Loading**: `ListRowSkeleton` ×3 on the left, `Skeleton` on the right.
- **Unavailable runtime**: `ErrorState` "A equipe não está disponível nesta versão" instead of a bare Notice.

## 10. Accessibility and keyboard

- The member list is a `listbox`; the detail is a labelled region ("Frontend, Desenvolvedor"). Focus returns to the
  member row after any action in the detail, and to the trigger when the dialog closes.
- Status dots always have words beside them. Role and kind badges are text, not colour.
- `Ctrl+Shift+T` opens Team (F35); inside, `Alt+N` adds a member, `/` focuses the message composer of the selected
  member.

## 13. Acceptance (S7.5 row)

- From an empty team, the person creates 6 members and connects each one to a different pane or conversation
  **without leaving the dialog** (picker), in under 3 minutes in the HML run.
- Each member shows which session it is bound to and opens it in one click.
- Picking a coordinator asks first (Cancel focused) and the badge agrees in Team, OxeChat and the S7 central.
- A delivery paused by the app shows as paused with its reason and appears in the activity center.
- Archive can be undone and restored. No English in pt-BR; no raw `Error` text; light/dark; keyboard only.
- The team runs a full day only through Team (S7.5's own acceptance in the roadmap).
